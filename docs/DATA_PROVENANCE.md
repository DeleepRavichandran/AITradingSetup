# Data provenance

## What data exists in this project

| Dataset | Real? | Source | Licence | In the repo? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `synthetic_v1` | **No — generated** | `nseql.data.fixtures.generate_fixture`, seed 20240101 | none needed | No (regenerated by `nseql fixtures`) |
| `tests/fixtures/*.csv` | **No — made-up values** in the exchange file layout | hand-written | none needed | Yes |

There is **no real market data** in this repository or its test suite. No price, premium, fill or
result presented anywhere came from NSE or a vendor.

### The synthetic fixture

12 equities across sectors (one delisted mid-sample, one listed late), a 1:2 split, nine
dividends, an equal-weighted index, monthly index futures and — from 2022 — monthly option chains
priced with Black-Scholes on a skewed, regime-dependent volatility surface, plus 1-minute bars for
two symbols. Weekdays only; no exchange holidays. Returns come from a three-state volatility
regime model with built-in return persistence in several names.

**Consequence:** trend and breakout rules do well on it *by construction*. A strategy "passing"
on the fixture is evidence that the pipeline runs, not that the idea has merit.

## Bringing in real data

No unauthorised scraping, and no redistribution. Two supported routes:

1. **Files you already hold.** `nseql import-bhavcopy <dataset_id> <files…> --licence "<basis>"`
   reads NSE end-of-day files (legacy CM, legacy F&O with lot sizes supplied, and UDiFF) from
   disk. It never downloads. Each file is copied byte-for-byte to `raw/` with its SHA-256;
   dropped rows and derived series are recorded in the manifest's audit list.
2. **A broker or vendor API you subscribe to.** `KiteHistorical` (unverified) or
   `import_csv` for vendor exports. Credentials come from environment variables only.

After importing: open *Data Quality & Coverage*, resolve errors, then supply corporate actions
(bhavcopies are unadjusted) and a holiday calendar. Check your licence before sharing any report
that embeds prices.

Derivative symbols are built as `<UNDERLYING><YY><MON><DD>FUT` / `…<STRIKE><CE|PE>`. Index levels
are taken from the `UndrlygPric` column of UDiFF F&O rows as a close-only series (recorded as
derived). Assumption to confirm on your first real file: UDiFF `TtlTradgVol` is in units, not lots.

### `rules/market_structure_in.json` — structural timeline

25 dated events (venue and product launches, settlement-cycle changes, tax changes, margin
framework, expiry rules). **It is a curated reference list, not a history of the Indian market and
not a news archive.** Only the three STT entries from 2023 onward are marked verified; the rest
were written from memory and are flagged `verified: false` in the file and in the dashboard.
Real-data backtests list the structural breaks inside their window as warnings.

### News and events

None shipped. `EventStore` is the ingestion and as-of query layer for a feed you license. A
complete, timestamped news history for Indian markets is a commercial data product; it cannot be
reconstructed from a model's memory without inventing it, so it has not been.
